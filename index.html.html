Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser on your device.

What formats can I export?

Vertical 9:16, square 1:1 and wide 16:9, as MP4 or WebM depending on your browser.

Does it caption what is said?

Not yet. You add hook text, and speech captions are planned.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly or yearly with no lock-in.
